DizyTV 0.1.18-alpha Windows installer — included components

The installer contains these private, verified playback and transcription
components, even if the PC has older or separate VLC/FFmpeg installations:

  - VideoLAN VLC 3.0.24 x64 private runtime, codec/protocol/audio/video plugins,
    and original legal notices (LGPL/GPL and module-dependent terms)
  - FFmpeg 8.1.2 essentials executable (original verified GPL build)
  - Vosk 0.3.40 offline transcription runtime (Apache 2.0)
  - Small English US Vosk 0.15 model (Apache 2.0)

The VLC/FFmpeg and Vosk files are packaged inside DizyTV, not installed
globally. VLC is used to play compatible clear HTTP/HLS streams. Vosk uses
CPU/memory only when local subtitles are enabled. No Windows system PATH,
existing VLC installation or separately installed FFmpeg is modified.

OPTIONAL: The unchecked Ace Stream installation task may download the
original third-party Ace installer directly from the official publisher
over HTTPS, verify its trusted publisher identity, and show its unchanged
installer/EULA. Ace uses P2P bandwidth; it is not required for ordinary
HTTP/HLS. If verification fails or the user cancels, DizyTV remains usable.
DizyTV does not modify or redistribute the proprietary Ace software.

These are unreleased development-installer notes until native CI, licence
compliance review and human playback checks pass. DizyTV cannot bypass DRM,
provider authentication or geographical restrictions.
